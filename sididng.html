Siding Installation & Exterior Renovation

A sharper exterior starts with the right siding product, the right prep, and the right details.

Siding installation is more than replacing the outside layer of the house. A strong siding project considers sheathing conditions, weather exposure, house wrap, trim transitions, window details, fascia, soffits, entry points, color direction, and how the finished exterior connects to the architecture of the home.

Diversified Plus provides siding installation, siding replacement, Hardie siding installation, fiber-cement siding guidance, exterior trim work, composite siding discussions, wood siding options, vinyl siding upgrades, and broader exterior renovation support across Westchester County, Putnam County, and Southern Connecticut.

Fiber-Cement & Hardie Siding

Fiber-cement siding built for durability, curb appeal, and lower-maintenance exterior performance.

Fiber-cement siding is a strong choice for homeowners who want a refined exterior that can perform better than many traditional materials. James Hardie and Hardiplank siding products are often selected for durability, fire resistance benefits, insect resistance, weather performance, and long-term curb appeal.

Diversified Plus helps homeowners compare siding profiles, color direction, trim details, exposure, maintenance expectations, and how fiber-cement siding fits the property’s architecture. The result should feel protective, clean, and visually balanced — not just newly covered.

Fiber-cement siding scopes can include
  • James Hardie siding and Hardiplank product guidance
  • Lap siding, panel siding, trim, corners, fascia, and soffit coordination
  • Exterior color planning, contrast trim, and ColorPlus® finish discussions
  • House wrap, weather-facing details, and long-term maintenance planning

Siding Replacement

Replace worn exterior materials with a cleaner, better-protected siding system.

Old siding can hide moisture damage, poor flashing, failed trim, soft sheathing, fading, warping, and areas where pests or water have found access. A siding replacement project gives homeowners the opportunity to improve the appearance of the home while addressing what is happening underneath the exterior layer.

Diversified Plus reviews siding condition, trim condition, weather exposure, windows, doors, corners, penetrations, and repair needs before the project moves forward. That review helps create a better scope and a more accurate siding installation plan.

Questions

Siding installation questions answered.

How much does siding replacement cost?

Siding replacement cost depends on the size of the home, material selection, trim complexity, number of stories, access, waste removal, repairs, and whether soffits, fascia, exterior trim, or moisture damage are included in the scope.

Do you install Hardie siding or fiber-cement siding?

Yes. Diversified Plus can help homeowners compare James Hardie, Hardiplank, and fiber-cement siding options for durability, curb appeal, weather resistance, and long-term maintenance expectations.

What siding material is best for my home?

The best siding material depends on the home’s style, exposure, budget, maintenance goals, durability needs, color direction, and how much exterior trim or repair work is required.

Can siding replacement improve curb appeal?

Yes. New siding can dramatically improve curb appeal when the profile, color, trim, corners, fascia, soffits, window details, and exterior transitions are planned together.

Do you provide siding services in New Rochelle, Mamaroneck, Bedford, and nearby towns?

Yes. Diversified Plus supports siding replacement and exterior renovation throughout Westchester, Putnam, and Southern Connecticut, including New Rochelle, Mamaroneck, Bedford, Thornwood, Larchmont, North Salem, Lake Waccabuc, Greenwich, Stamford, and nearby communities.

Will old siding reveal hidden repairs?

Sometimes. Once old siding is removed, hidden moisture damage, soft sheathing, flashing issues, trim deterioration, or repair needs may appear. A good siding scope accounts for this possibility before the project begins.
