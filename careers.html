Careers At Diversified Plus

A construction career built around precision, trust, and better work.

Diversified Plus is a residential construction and renovation company serving Westchester County, Putnam County, and Southern Connecticut. Our work includes kitchens, bathrooms, full renovations, additions, siding, decks, pool decks, pergolas, gazebos, and outdoor living projects.

We are building a team of skilled, dependable, detail-minded people who care about more than getting the job done. We care about how the job is planned, how the site is protected, how clients are treated, how trades are coordinated, and how the final details come together.

Our Work Standard

We hire for skill, but we keep people because of character.

Construction requires judgment. Every day brings real decisions about sequence, protection, quality, time, and client expectations. The right person for Diversified Plus understands that great work is not only technical — it is also professional.

We value people who communicate early, respect the jobsite, keep tools and materials organized, ask smart questions, and take pride in doing work correctly even when the detail is small.

What matters here
  • Reliable attendance and strong jobsite habits
  • Respectful communication with clients, teammates, and trades
  • Clean work areas, careful prep, and pride in finish details
  • Problem-solving, accountability, safety, and willingness to learn

What We Value

The habits that make someone a strong fit.

01

Clean Work

Homes are not construction zones by default. We protect floors, organize materials, manage dust, and leave spaces with care.

02

Clear Communication

Clients and teammates should not have to guess. We communicate delays, questions, changes, and next steps early.

03

Measured Craft

We value straight lines, aligned trim, accurate cuts, clean transitions, durable assemblies, and details that last.

04

Accountability

Good teams own the work. We look for people who solve problems, ask for help, and take responsibility.

05

Respect

Respect for the home, client, schedule, coworkers, trades, and neighborhood is part of the job.

06

Growth Mindset

Construction is always teaching. We value candidates who want to improve their craft, leadership, and judgment.
