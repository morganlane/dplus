Bathroom Renovation

A better bathroom starts behind the wall, under the tile, and inside the plan.

A bathroom remodel is one of the most technical renovations in the home. The room is compact, water-heavy, and used every day, which means every choice has to work harder: waterproofing, drainage, ventilation, lighting, tile layout, fixture placement, storage, glass, trim, and finish alignment.

Diversified Plus builds bathroom renovation projects for homeowners across Westchester County, Putnam County, and Southern Connecticut, including primary bathroom remodels, hall bath renovations, powder room updates, tub-to-shower conversions, walk-in shower remodeling, vanity replacement, tile bathroom renovation, and full gut bath remodels.

Primary Bathroom Remodeling

Primary baths designed for routine, storage, lighting, and a calmer start to the day.

A primary bathroom remodel can change the way the home feels every morning and every night. Diversified Plus helps homeowners think through vanity size, sink layout, shower size, tub removal, linen storage, lighting, ventilation, mirrors, tile direction, and fixture finish.

The goal is a bathroom that feels refined without sacrificing practical use. That means clean sightlines, durable surfaces, strong waterproofing, comfortable lighting, useful storage, accessible fixtures, and finishes that belong with the rest of the home.

Primary bath scopes can include
  • Double vanity replacement, storage planning, and countertop coordination
  • Walk-in shower design, custom glass, tile niches, benches, and shower fixtures
  • Freestanding tub removal or replacement, lighting, mirrors, and ventilation upgrades
  • Full bathroom gut renovation with plumbing, electrical, tile, and finish coordination

Walk-In Showers & Tub-to-Shower Conversions

Shower remodeling that balances waterproofing, comfort, and clean visual detail.

A shower conversion is one of the most requested bathroom upgrades because it improves everyday comfort and can make the room feel larger, cleaner, and easier to use. Diversified Plus plans shower conversions around the existing plumbing, waterproofing requirements, tile layout, glass openings, niche placement, slope, drainage, lighting, and fixture height.

Whether the project is a tub-to-shower conversion, a walk-in shower remodel, a curbless-shower discussion, or a full tile shower replacement, the hidden construction matters as much as the visible tile.

WaterproofingSubstrate, membranes, shower pan planning, and wet-area detailing.

Bathroom remodel cost depends on the room size, age of the plumbing, tile selection, vanity and fixture choices, shower complexity, waterproofing requirements, ventilation needs, and whether walls, floors, or layouts are changing.

Questions

Bathroom renovation questions answered.

How much does a bathroom renovation cost?

Bathroom renovation cost depends on the room size, fixture choices, tile selection, waterproofing requirements, plumbing conditions, ventilation needs, and whether the layout is changing. A powder room update is very different from a full gut primary bathroom remodel.

Do you handle walk-in shower conversions?

Yes. Diversified Plus can support tub-to-shower conversion, walk-in shower remodeling, waterproofing, tile, shower glass, fixture placement, niches, benches, and drainage coordination.

Do I need permits for bathroom remodeling?

Permits may be required when plumbing, electrical, structural changes, ventilation, or larger renovation scopes are involved. Diversified Plus helps clarify what is required for the project and location.

Can you remodel small bathrooms or powder rooms?

Yes. Compact bathrooms and powder rooms can benefit from better lighting, wall finishes, vanity replacement, tile upgrades, fixture updates, ventilation, mirrors, and storage planning.

Do you provide bathroom remodeling in New Rochelle, Mamaroneck, Bedford, and nearby towns?

Yes. Diversified Plus supports bath renovation and bathroom remodeling throughout Westchester, Putnam, and Southern Connecticut, including New Rochelle, Mamaroneck, Bedford, Thornwood, Larchmont, North Salem, Lake Waccabuc, Greenwich, Stamford, and nearby communities.

What makes a bathroom remodel feel more premium?

Premium bath remodeling is often found in the quiet details: better lighting, aligned tile, thoughtful niches, cleaner glass lines, strong ventilation, useful storage, durable surfaces, and a room that feels calm instead of overdesigned.
