About Diversified Plus

A construction company built around trust, accountability, and practical field experience.

Diversified Plus Construction provides renovation and construction services with a clear commitment to honest relationships. The company’s original promise — “Building Relationships Through Honest Contracting” — reflects how the team approaches each job: with communication, respect for the homeowner’s budget, and a focus on doing the work correctly.

The company brings decades of experience across residential construction, including carpentry, construction, plumbing, electrical, HVAC coordination, architectural support, and permit assistance. That broad capability allows the team to approach a project with the full picture in mind, not just one trade or one phase of the build.

Founder Oversight

A project point person from the first meeting through completion.

Diversified Plus Construction was founded by Jack Ricotta, a father of four and an active member of the community. With more than 20 years in the home building industry and a specialization in custom millwork and trim, Jack brings both field experience and a detail-oriented eye to the company’s work.

Jack is the first person clients meet and serves as the point person on the project. His role is hands-on: keeping the project within budget, maintaining schedule awareness, coordinating subcontractors, arranging inspections, and providing periodic updates as the job progresses.

Diversified Plus Goes Green

Responsible building choices that reduce waste, lower maintenance, and support long-term value.

Diversified Plus is committed to using environmentally safe and eco-friendly products. The company helps clients consider green alternatives when reviewing construction options, especially when those materials can reduce long-term maintenance and support more responsible use of resources.

Green building choices can reduce waste going to landfills and demonstrate a commitment to environmental stewardship and social responsibility. The company’s green product guidance includes low-maintenance, affordable, and recyclable building products for exterior, deck, and renovation applications.

Why Green?

Traditional materials still have a place, but composite and fiber-cement alternatives can often provide longer-lasting use and far less maintenance than regular wood.

Material Guidance

Green product categories.

  • Fiber cement products such as Hardiplank
  • Composite decking products
  • Low-maintenance exterior materials
  • Recyclable building products
  • Alternatives to traditional wood when appropriate
  • Product options that may align with available incentives
