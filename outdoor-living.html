Pool Decks, Pergolas & Gazebos

Backyard structures that make outdoor living feel planned, comfortable, and complete.

A successful outdoor living project considers more than the surface underfoot. Pool decks need moisture-aware material choices, safe transitions, foot traffic planning, lounge zones, and clean connections to the home. Pergolas and gazebos need proportion, shade, placement, structural clarity, and a visual relationship to the architecture around them.

Diversified Plus provides pool deck construction, composite pool decks, pergola construction, gazebo construction, backyard shade structures, outdoor living upgrades, railings, stairs, privacy screens, deck lighting, outdoor seating areas, and full backyard renovation planning across Westchester County, Putnam County, and Southern Connecticut.

Pool Deck Construction

Pool decks designed for wet zones, movement, comfort, and a cleaner backyard experience.

Pool decks need to perform differently than a standard deck or patio. They need to consider water exposure, barefoot comfort, slip awareness, drainage, furniture areas, pool access, step-down conditions, privacy, and how people move between the house, yard, and pool.

Diversified Plus helps homeowners plan pool decks around composite decking, railings, stairs, lounge zones, shade, fencing transitions, storage, lighting, and the details that make a poolside area feel finished instead of improvised.

Pool deck scopes can include
  • Pool deck construction, poolside decking, and composite pool deck planning
  • Moisture-aware material guidance, deck board direction, fascia, and trim details
  • Steps, railings, access paths, lounging zones, outdoor lighting, and privacy
  • Connections between the home, patio, yard, pool, and outdoor gathering spaces

Pergolas & Gazebos

Shade structures that make outdoor rooms feel architectural, useful, and complete.

Pergolas and gazebos can change how a backyard is used. They help define seating areas, create shade, improve privacy, anchor outdoor dining, and make a pool deck or patio feel like a complete outdoor room.

Diversified Plus plans pergola construction and gazebo construction around proportion, placement, structural needs, materials, sightlines, privacy, lighting, and how the structure relates to the home, pool, patio, deck, and landscape.

Questions

Pool deck, pergola, and gazebo questions answered.

How much does pool deck construction cost?

Pool deck cost depends on size, materials, access, existing conditions, moisture exposure, stairs, railings, trim, decking pattern, demolition, lighting, drainage, and how the deck connects to the pool, home, and yard.

Do you build pergolas and gazebos?

Yes. Diversified Plus can support pergola construction, gazebo construction, shade structures, covered seating areas, privacy screens, lighting, and backyard layout planning.

What materials are best for a pool deck?

The best pool deck material depends on exposure, maintenance goals, budget, comfort, slip awareness, appearance, drainage, and how the pool deck connects to other outdoor living areas. Composite decking is often considered for lower-maintenance performance.

Can you combine a pool deck with a pergola or gazebo?

Yes. Many outdoor living projects combine pool decks, pergolas, gazebos, privacy screens, outdoor seating, lighting, stairs, railings, and lounge zones into one coordinated backyard plan.

Do you provide pool deck and pergola services in New Rochelle, Mamaroneck, Bedford, and nearby towns?

Yes. Diversified Plus supports pool decks, pergolas, gazebos, and outdoor living renovation throughout Westchester, Putnam, and Southern Connecticut, including New Rochelle, Mamaroneck, Bedford, Thornwood, Larchmont, North Salem, Lake Waccabuc, Greenwich, Stamford, and nearby communities.

What makes an outdoor living project feel more premium?

Premium outdoor living is often found in the quiet details: clean deck board direction, strong shade placement, aligned railings, comfortable stair paths, purposeful lighting, privacy planning, and a layout that feels connected to the home.
