Whole-Home Remodeling

Renovate the home as one cohesive project, not a set of disconnected rooms.

Whole-home remodeling is about seeing the house as a complete environment. The kitchen should relate to the living areas. Bathrooms should support the bedrooms they serve. Flooring, trim, lighting, doors, hardware, paint, and exterior details should work together instead of feeling like separate decisions made at different times.

Diversified Plus helps homeowners review the existing layout, identify pain points, prioritize budget, coordinate trades, and plan the renovation in a sequence that supports a stronger finished result.

Full renovation scopes can include
  • Kitchen remodeling, bathroom remodeling, flooring, trim, lighting, paint, and finish updates
  • Wall openings, room reconfiguration, structural coordination, and circulation improvements
  • Electrical, plumbing, HVAC, insulation, drywall, interior doors, and millwork coordination
  • Basement finishing, additions, exterior upgrades, and multi-room renovation sequencing

Interior Renovations & Layout Changes

Improve how the home moves, feels, and functions from room to room.

Interior renovation can change the character of the home without necessarily changing its footprint. Removing walls, opening kitchens, improving stair circulation, refinishing floors, upgrading lighting, modernizing bathrooms, finishing basements, and aligning trim details can dramatically improve daily life.

Diversified Plus plans interior renovation work around how each decision affects the next. That means a flooring change may affect trim, a kitchen opening may affect structure, and new lighting may require electrical planning. A complete renovation should reduce conflicts, not create them.

Questions

Full home renovation questions answered.

How much does a full home renovation cost?

Cost depends on the size of the home, number of rooms, kitchen and bathroom scope, layout changes, structural work, material selections, utility updates, permits, finishes, access, and whether the home is occupied during construction.

What is included in a full renovation?

A full renovation can include kitchens, bathrooms, flooring, trim, lighting, paint, doors, wall openings, structural work, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, basement finishing, additions, exterior updates, and finish carpentry.

Do I need permits for whole-home remodeling?

Permits may be required when electrical, plumbing, HVAC, structural changes, additions, layout changes, or major reconstruction are involved. Diversified Plus helps clarify what is required for the specific project and location.

Can I live in the home during a full renovation?

Sometimes, but it depends on the scope. Kitchens, bathrooms, utilities, dust, noise, and access may make living in the home difficult. The plan should account for protection, temporary routines, and realistic construction phases.

Do you provide full renovation services in New Rochelle, Mamaroneck, Bedford, and nearby towns?

Yes. Diversified Plus supports full home renovation throughout Westchester, Putnam, and Southern Connecticut, including New Rochelle, Mamaroneck, Bedford, Thornwood, Larchmont, North Salem, Lake Waccabuc, Greenwich, Stamford, and nearby communities.

What makes a full renovation feel more premium?

Premium renovation is often found in consistency: aligned finishes, better lighting, thoughtful flow, balanced materials, clean trim, cohesive hardware, durable surfaces, and decisions that feel connected across the entire home.
