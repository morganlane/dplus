Deck building is about structure, outdoor comfort, material performance, and the way the deck connects back to the home. Diversified Plus helps homeowners plan outdoor living spaces that feel intentional, useful, and built for long-term value.

Deck Building & Outdoor Living

A better deck starts with the way you want to live outside.

A successful deck project considers more than boards and railings. The design should account for how people gather, where stairs land, how the deck connects to the kitchen or living room, what views should be preserved, how privacy is handled, where furniture will sit, how sun and shade move, and how the structure will perform over time.

Diversified Plus provides deck building, deck replacement, composite decking, Trex deck installation guidance, Azek decking, Fiberon decking, TimberTech decking, pool decks, porch construction, pergolas, railings, stairs, screened spaces, and full outdoor living renovation support across Westchester County, Putnam County, and Southern Connecticut.

Composite Decking

Composite decking for lower-maintenance outdoor living with a cleaner finish.

Composite decking is a strong option for homeowners who want outdoor living space without the same maintenance cycle as traditional wood. Products from Trex, Azek, Fiberon, and TimberTech can help reduce staining, sealing, splintering, and routine upkeep while giving the deck a refined appearance.

Diversified Plus helps homeowners compare boards, colors, textures, railing systems, trim details, hidden fasteners, stair design, and how composite decking fits the architecture of the home. The goal is a deck that feels built-in, not added on.

Composite decking scopes can include
  • Trex, Azek, Fiberon, and TimberTech product guidance
  • Hidden fasteners, trim boards, fascia, picture framing, and board direction
  • Railings, stairs, landings, gates, privacy screens, and lighting discussions
  • Pool decks, backyard decks, porches, and outdoor entertaining platforms

Deck Replacement

Replace an aging deck with a safer, cleaner, and more useful outdoor space.

Older decks can show signs of wear through loose railings, failing stairs, soft framing, weathered boards, poor drainage, unstable landings, or layouts that no longer fit how the family uses the home. A deck replacement project is a chance to improve structure, code compliance, traffic flow, and curb appeal at the same time.

Diversified Plus reviews the existing deck framing, connection points, footings, railings, stair geometry, joist condition, ledger details, drainage, and finish goals before moving forward. That review helps create a better scope and a stronger outdoor living plan.

Questions

Deck construction questions answered.

How much does deck construction cost?

Deck construction cost depends on size, material, height, framing, footings, stairs, railings, access, demolition, board pattern, fascia, lighting, privacy, and whether the project includes a pergola, pool deck, porch, or screened space.

Do you build composite decks?

Yes. Diversified Plus can help homeowners compare composite decking options such as Trex, Azek, Fiberon, and TimberTech for lower-maintenance performance, durability, board color, railing compatibility, and long-term value.

Should I repair or replace my old deck?

That depends on the condition of the framing, footings, ledger, stairs, railings, decking boards, and overall safety. Sometimes targeted repairs work; other times a full replacement is the better long-term investment.

Can you add railings, stairs, lighting, or a pergola?

Yes. Deck projects can include railings, stair runs, landings, gates, privacy screens, lighting, pergolas, porches, and outdoor living details depending on the layout and scope.

Do you provide deck services in New Rochelle, Mamaroneck, Bedford, and nearby towns?

Yes. Diversified Plus supports deck construction and outdoor living renovation throughout Westchester, Putnam, and Southern Connecticut, including New Rochelle, Mamaroneck, Bedford, Thornwood, Larchmont, North Salem, Lake Waccabuc, Greenwich, Stamford, and nearby communities.

What makes a deck feel more premium?

A premium deck is often defined by quiet details: proportional stairs, aligned railings, clean fascia, thoughtful board direction, hidden fasteners, useful lighting, privacy planning, and a strong connection to the home.
