What We Do

One contractor. Multiple disciplines. One clear standard from first conversation to final detail.

Diversified Plus is a full-service residential contractor specializing in additions, renovations, kitchens, baths, decking, siding, and exterior work. The company brings together carpentry, construction management, mechanical coordination, architectural support, permit assistance, and finish detail under one practical process.

The result is a service model built for homeowners who want a contractor to help clarify the project, coordinate the moving parts, manage the field reality, and finish the work with care. Whether the scope is a kitchen, an exterior transformation, or a full residential renovation, the same standard applies: clear planning, responsible materials, and disciplined execution.

01 / Additions & Remodeling

Expand the home while keeping the architecture, flow, and finish language connected.

Additions are about more than square footage. A successful addition has to respect the existing home, improve how the property functions, and solve the practical construction challenges that come with tying new work into old structure. Diversified Plus helps homeowners move from the early idea to a defined scope, then through design coordination, construction, and finishing.

Projects can include powder room additions, garage expansions, den additions, second-story additions, kitchen additions, enclosed porch additions, lake house additions, and complete remodels. The team focuses on proportion, roofline integration, exterior continuity, interior circulation, utilities, inspections, and finish details so the final space does not feel like an afterthought.

Common scopes
  • Second-story and multi-floor additions
  • Garage additions and expansions
  • Kitchen additions and connected living space
  • Basement finishing, in-law rooms, nanny rooms, and total remodels

02 / Residential Renovations

Renovations that turn existing rooms into better-functioning spaces without losing control of the details.

Renovation work often involves more hidden complexity than new construction. Existing framing, electrical, plumbing, HVAC, flooring transitions, access limitations, and occupied-home realities all shape the final result. Diversified Plus plans renovations with those conditions in mind so the project can move with fewer surprises.

Large-scale interior renovation work may include kitchens, bathrooms, hardwood floors, basements, mechanical coordination, fine finishing, and construction management. Exterior renovation work can include decking, screened-in porches, gazebos, cabanas, roofing, and siding. The goal is to create a finished space that feels intentional, useful, and aligned with how the home is actually lived in.

PlanningScope, schedule, selections, trade coordination, and access.
ConstructionCarpentry, mechanical coordination, installation, and field oversight.
FinishTrim, transitions, surfaces, hardware, alignment, and final detail.

03 / Kitchens

Kitchen renovations built around daily movement, storage, lighting, and finish restraint.

A kitchen renovation succeeds when the beautiful parts and the functional parts are planned together. Diversified Plus looks at how the kitchen connects to the rest of the home, where people gather, how storage should work, what lighting is needed, where appliances belong, and how surfaces and cabinetry should be coordinated.

The construction plan can include demolition, layout changes, cabinetry, millwork, electrical planning, plumbing, lighting, flooring transitions, countertops, backsplash, finish carpentry, and appliance coordination. The goal is a kitchen that feels elevated but still works hard for everyday use.

Kitchen priorities
  • Cabinet rhythm, storage, and built-in function
  • Lighting temperature, task zones, and appliance placement
  • Material transitions between floors, counters, walls, and trim
  • Open-concept changes with practical structural coordination

04 / Baths

Bathroom renovations where waterproofing, layout, ventilation, and tile detail matter just as much as style.

Bathrooms are compact, technical rooms. Plumbing, ventilation, waterproofing, framing, tile layout, fixture placement, and lighting all have to work together in a tight footprint. Diversified Plus approaches bath projects with attention to what is behind the wall as much as what is visible at the end.

The work can include primary bath renovations, hall baths, powder rooms, showers, tubs, vanities, tile installation, glass, storage, lighting, ventilation, and finish coordination. The result should feel clean, durable, and comfortable without sacrificing practical performance.

WaterproofingProper prep behind tile and wet areas.
Tile detailLayout, spacing, edge work, and material transitions.
Daily comfortLighting, storage, ventilation, and fixture access.

05 / Decking & Outdoor Living

Decks, porches, and outdoor structures designed for comfort, safety, and lower-maintenance living.

Decking is both structural and experiential. It has to feel good to use, connect properly to the home, hold up to weather, and meet safety expectations. Diversified Plus works with homeowners to understand the intended use, design options, material choices, access points, color direction, railings, stairs, and the maintenance expectations of the finished space.

Decking services can include side porches, front-covered porches, backyard decks, multi-level decks, pool decks, hot-tub decks, pergolas, gazebos, and cabanas. Material options can include traditional woods such as mahogany, cedar, and pressure-treated pine, as well as composite and environmentally safe materials from manufacturers such as Azek, Trex, Fiberon, and TimberTech.

Outdoor scopes
  • Backyard decks, front porches, covered porches, and side porches
  • Multi-level decks, pool decks, hot-tub decks, pergolas, and gazebos
  • Composite decking with low-maintenance color and finish options
  • Certified installer coordination for manufacturer-backed systems

06 / Siding & Exteriors

Exterior siding and trim work that sharpens curb appeal while protecting the structure.

Siding is one of the most visible investments a homeowner can make, but it is also a performance decision. Diversified Plus provides consulting and installation for siding options ranging from traditional vinyl and wood to composite siding and James Hardie fiber-cement products.

Hardie Siding ColorPlus® and fiber-cement siding are valued for color consistency, durability, insect resistance, fire-safety benefits, and long-term exterior performance. Diversified Plus helps clients consider material appearance, maintenance expectations, protection, trim details, color, and overall architectural balance before installation begins.

MaterialsVinyl, wood, composite, Hardie, and fiber-cement options.
ProtectionWeather-facing details, trim, and long-term durability.
ValueCurb appeal, lower maintenance, and exterior performance.

07 / Green Product Guidance

Eco-conscious product decisions that support durability, lower maintenance, and responsible building.

Diversified Plus is committed to offering eco-friendly and environmentally safe products for the home. In practical terms, that means helping clients compare traditional building choices with lower-maintenance, recyclable, composite, or fiber-cement alternatives when those materials make sense for the scope.

The company’s green guidance can apply to decking, siding, exterior renovations, and broader renovation decisions. Composite decking, fiber-cement siding, and low-maintenance exterior materials can help reduce upkeep, increase durability, and reduce waste over time when selected and installed properly.

Product examples
  • Trex, Azek, Fiberon, and TimberTech decking systems
  • James Hardie siding and ColorPlus® technology
  • Composite, recyclable, and environmentally safe material options
  • Low-maintenance exterior systems for long-term value

How Services Are Delivered

A service process designed to keep the work clear, coordinated, and accountable.

01Discovery

02Scope
