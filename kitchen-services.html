Kitchen Remodeling Process

A comprehensive approach that starts before demolition.

Our approach to residential kitchen remodeling is rooted in planning and field coordination. Whether the home is in Scarsdale, Greenwich, Stamford, Westport, Darien, Rye, White Plains, Mahopac, Brewster, or another nearby community, we re-evaluate movement, light, appliance placement, storage, electrical capacity, plumbing paths, and the finish details that make the room feel resolved.

Before construction begins, the layout is clarified with planning, measurements, cabinet zones, finish direction, backsplash concepts, lighting placement, appliance requirements, and floor transition details.
We coordinate demolition, plumbing rerouting, electrical upgrades, ventilation paths, gas line requirements, and any structural adjustments needed for modern appliances and open layouts.
Our installation process focuses on cabinetry alignment, island placement, countertop integration, lighting trim-out, hardware, flooring, backsplash lines, cleanup, and final finishing touches.
